# Nexus — Internal Task and Project Management Platform

**Role:** Product Manager  
**Date in source portfolio:** April 2026  
**Team:** Engineering, Design and Product  
**Status in source portfolio:** Shipped

## Overview

Nexus is an internal task and project management platform created to bring project boards, sprint planning, support tickets and software development tracking into one workspace.

The goal was to give cross-functional teams a more unified way to coordinate work, reduce context switching and improve visibility across delivery activities.

## Problem

The source portfolio describes a growing need for a shared internal workspace. Project information and delivery activities needed to be brought together so teams could plan work, manage support requests and follow software projects through their lifecycle.

## My role and contribution

As Product Manager, the source portfolio describes my involvement across end-to-end product development:

- Led product discovery and requirements definition.
- Collaborated with engineering, design and product stakeholders.
- Prioritized features and translated needs into requirements.
- Defined acceptance criteria and worked with engineering and QA.
- Supported rapid prototyping, delivery and launch.

## Product capabilities

The platform brings together project boards, sprint planning and tracking, support ticket management and triage, software development project tracking, user and project visibility, and analytics to support operational oversight.

## Intended value

The portfolio describes the intended and reported qualitative benefits as better visibility across projects and teams, improved sprint predictability, faster support ticket resolution, stronger cross-functional collaboration and less context switching.

**Measurement note:** The source portfolio does not provide baseline and post-launch figures for these outcomes. No percentage improvements or time-savings figures are claimed in this case study.
