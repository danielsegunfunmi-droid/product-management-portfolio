# Reward System — Engagement and Gamification Platform

**Role:** Product Manager  
**Date in source portfolio:** 2025  
**Focus:** Engagement and retention  
**Surfaces:** Educator dashboard and global leaderboard  
**Status in source portfolio:** Shipped

## Overview

The Reward System is a gamified engagement layer designed to encourage adoption and sustained use of platform features. It recognizes meaningful user actions through points, badges, milestones and leaderboards.

## Problem

The source portfolio frames the core challenge as improving engagement and driving deeper, more consistent adoption of existing platform features.

The product needed to make progress visible and recognize meaningful activity without making the reward mechanics feel manipulative.

## My role and contribution

As Product Manager, the source portfolio describes my contribution as defining reward logic and milestone triggers, working with engineering and design on progression tiers, translating the engagement goal into product requirements, and supporting the development of educator-facing and platform-wide reward experiences.

## Product experience

The platform includes a reward dashboard where educators can view progress and achievements, points and badges tied to milestones, progression tiers that recognize user activity, and a global leaderboard that highlights top performers.

## Intended value

The product was designed to encourage discovery and adoption of platform features, reinforce positive usage habits, make progress and achievement visible, recognize users for meaningful activity, and strengthen ongoing engagement with the platform.

**Measurement note:** The source portfolio mentions thousands of users on the global leaderboard but does not provide a verified adoption baseline, post-launch comparison or causal impact analysis. This case study therefore avoids claiming a specific uplift in engagement or retention.
