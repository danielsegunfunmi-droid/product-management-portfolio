# Internal Loan Management — Staff Loan and Repayment Tracking

**Role:** Product Manager  
**Date in source portfolio:** 2026  
**Users:** Staff and Finance leadership  
**Focus:** HR and finance operations  
**Status in source portfolio:** Shipped

## Overview

Internal Loan Management is an internal HR and finance product for managing staff loan applications, approvals, active loans and monthly repayments.

The product brings the process into one place, replacing scattered spreadsheets and manual approval chains described in the source portfolio.

## Problem

The process required a clearer way to manage staff eligibility, applications, approval routing, repayment schedules and outstanding balances. Staff needed visibility into their loan eligibility and application history. Finance needed an operational view of pending applications, active loans and repayments due.

## My role and contribution

As Product Manager, the source portfolio describes my contribution as defining staff eligibility logic, specifying the application and approval flow, defining repayment schedule rules, working through the needs of staff and finance users, and structuring self-service and finance-facing workflows.

The source describes eligibility as allowing staff to request up to a set percentage of annual salary based on tenure. The exact percentage is not specified in the portfolio source and is intentionally not inferred here.

## Product capabilities

**Staff self-service portal**
- View salary and tenure information.
- View maximum eligible loan amount.
- Submit loan applications.
- Review loan history.

**Finance dashboard**
- Review pending applications.
- Track active loans and outstanding balances.
- View amounts due for the month.
- Manage monthly repayment records.

**Repayment and audit workflow**
- Mark repayments as paid after payroll deduction.
- Export repayment records to CSV.
- Maintain an audit log of decisions and activity.

## Intended value

The product was designed to improve visibility and consistency across the loan lifecycle, reduce reliance on manual tracking and give staff and finance users clearer workflows.

**Measurement note:** The source portfolio does not provide quantified reductions in processing time, reconciliation effort or error rates. No numerical impact is claimed in this case study.
